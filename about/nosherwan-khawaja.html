Nosherwan Khawaja

Chief Information Officer

Nosherwan Khawaja is a finance and technology professional with over 14 years of experience across audit, advisory, financial management and business transformation. He combines a strong professional background in accounting and finance with hands-on involvement in technology, digital platforms, process automation and data-driven business solutions.

As CIO, he leads the organisation's technology and digital transformation agenda, focusing on building scalable systems, improving business processes, integrating technology with operations, and using data and automation to support better decision-making.

His experience spans financial services, manufacturing, retail, professional services and technology-enabled businesses, with particular focus on business process transformation, financial systems, automation, digital product development, controls and technology-enabled advisory.

He also brings extensive experience working with senior management and business owners, allowing him to bridge the gap between business strategy, finance and technology and translate business requirements into practical technology solutions.
