Leadership Spotlight

Ruqayya Hafeez

Chief Executive Officer · 25 years building & scaling education platforms

Ruqayya Hafeez is a seasoned EdTech and digital-transformation leader with over 20 years of experience designing, delivering and scaling technology-driven education solutions across the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Pakistan and Malaysia — aligning global best practices with local needs.

She was the driving force behind BEAMS, a state-of-the-art educational information system regarded as an industry benchmark, leading its product vision, roadmap and enterprise implementations for over 15 years. Her work spans solution architecture, programme & change management and institution-wide rollouts — including with FACTS, a US EdTech platform used by thousands of schools.

As CEO of Digi8, she now leads the development of next-generation EdTech and banking products with an AI-native, future-ready approach — embedding intelligence, automation and data-centric thinking into core platforms across education, governance and enterprise technology.
