One platform for every school conversation

From a single broadcast to thousands of parents to a one-to-one chat with a teacher — organised, tracked and reliable.

Digi8's Messaging System replaces scattered SMS blasts, WhatsApp groups and paper circulars with one governed platform. Schools send announcements, trigger automated alerts, and hold two-way conversations with staff and parents — with delivery tracked from send to read.

  • Automated alerts for attendance, fees, results and events

Key Features

One WhatsApp number for everything the school sends to parents and everything parents ask back.

Result Card Delivery

Upload marks from Excel or pull from the school ERP. Each student receives a branded PDF result card, with separate templates for parents and students.

Fee Voucher Push

Monthly fee vouchers go out automatically with the due date. Reminders follow before the due date and after it for unpaid accounts.

Two-Way Parent Queries

Parents reply on the same number to request a result, a fee voucher or an attendance summary. The system verifies the parent and answers within seconds.

Attendance and Performance Alerts

Same-day absence notices, low-attendance warnings and low-score alerts, sent to parents from rules the school configures.

Circulars and Announcements

Holiday notices, exam schedules, PTM invitations and event circulars sent to a whole campus, a grade or a single section.

Delivery Dashboard

Sent, delivered, read and failed counts per campaign, with a list of invalid or unreachable numbers for the office to correct.

How it works

No app for parents to install. Everything runs on the WhatsApp they already use.

1
Upload or Sync Data

The school uploads marks, fees or attendance in Excel, or the system reads them directly from the ERP or student information system.

2
Generate PDFs

Result cards and fee vouchers are generated on the school letterhead from approved templates, one per student.

3
Send on WhatsApp

Messages go out on the school's official WhatsApp Business number through Meta's Cloud API, with delivery tracking.

4
Answer Parent Replies

Parent replies are matched to the student record and answered automatically. Anything the system cannot answer is routed to the school office.

Security and Control

Student data stays under the school's control, and every message is traceable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do parents need to install an app?

No. Everything runs on the WhatsApp parents already use — there is no app to download and no account to create.

Can the school keep using its existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. The system runs on the school's own WhatsApp Business number, verified with Meta, and the existing WhatsApp Business App on that number can continue to be used for manual chat.

Which languages are supported?

Templates are submitted to Meta for approval in both English and Urdu as part of onboarding. Additional languages can be added on request.

Is it limited to results and fee vouchers?

No. It also sends attendance and performance alerts, circulars, exam schedules, PTM invitations and event notices, and it answers parent queries — all from the same number.

What happens if a parent asks something the system cannot answer?

The message is routed to the school office so a staff member can reply. Nothing is lost, and every thread stays logged against the student record.

What data does the school need to provide?

Student, parent and section data — uploaded once from Excel or integrated directly from the school's ERP or student information system, then kept in sync. Marks, fees and attendance flow in each cycle.

Can this be deployed on the school's own servers?

Yes. It can be hosted by Digi8 or deployed on the school's own infrastructure, with data hosted in a region the school selects.
