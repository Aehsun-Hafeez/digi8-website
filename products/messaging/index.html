Overview

One platform for every school conversation

From a single broadcast to thousands of parents to a one-to-one chat with a teacher — organised, tracked and reliable.

Digi8's Messaging System replaces scattered SMS blasts, WhatsApp groups and paper circulars with one governed platform. Schools send announcements, trigger automated alerts, and hold two-way conversations with staff and parents — with delivery tracked from send to read.

  • Broadcast announcements to a class, a group or the whole school
  • Automated alerts for attendance, fees, results and events
  • Two-way messaging between staff and parents
  • Delivery tracking, message templates and scheduled sends
