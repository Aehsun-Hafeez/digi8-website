Portfolio Management & Fund Investment

Put the fund to work — and share the returns fairly

A provident fund is a growing pool of capital that must be invested prudently and accounted for transparently. Digi8 PF invests the total fund pool as per your company policy and approved investment guidelines, tracks every holding, calculates the annual return, and distributes profit proportionately to members — a transparent, policy-compliant process that builds employee trust and meets regulatory requirements.

a

Fund

Employee and employer contributions accumulate every cycle into a single, member-level provident fund pool.

b

Investment

The total PF fund pool is invested as per company policy and approved investment guidelines, across a managed portfolio of instruments, to generate returns.

c

Share

Annual profit is calculated on the total fund from investment returns, then shared proportionately to each member based on their accumulated PF balance.

Transparent, policy-compliant profit distribution — building employee trust and regulatory compliance.

Portfolio management

Maintain a live view of the fund's holdings across every asset class — bank deposits, government securities, mutual funds, equities and term instruments — each governed by your approved investment guidelines, with cost, current value, maturity and yield captured for every position. Record buy and sell transactions, accruals and realised gains, and monitor the portfolio's overall return, exposure and liquidity from a single dashboard.

  • Consolidated view of every instrument and asset class
  • Cost vs. current value, maturity and yield per holding
  • Buy / sell, accrual and realised-gain tracking
  • Portfolio return, exposure and liquidity at a glance

Fund investment

The total fund pool is invested as per company policy and approved investment guidelines. The platform records each instrument's principal, profit rate and tenure, computes accrued and realised income from investment returns, and reconciles those returns back to the fund — so the annual profit members receive is traceable all the way to the underlying assets.

  • Invested per company policy & approved investment guidelines
  • Principal, profit rate and tenure tracked per instrument
  • Annual profit calculated on the total fund from returns
  • Profit distributed proportionately by accumulated balance
