Six core functions

Everything your provident fund needs

Each function is configurable to your fund's policy and integrated end to end.

01 Contributions

Employee Contribution

A fixed percentage deducted monthly from salary and credited to each employee's PF account.

02 Contributions

Employer Contribution

The company matches with a configurable percentage based on policy.

03 Returns

Profit / Interest

Annual returns calculated on the total fund and distributed proportionately to members.

04 Lending

Loan Management

Apply, approve and track PF loans — deductions handled via payroll integration.

05 Self-service

Employee Self-Service

A 24/7 ESS portal for balances, contribution history and applications.

06 Exit

Final Settlement

Automated payout on resignation, retirement or termination, with a full statement.

Operational workflow

Automated from contribution to settlement

The platform automates contributions, profit distribution, loan processing, employee portals and final settlements — all connected through your payroll system. When an employee exits, their settlement is computed and paid out automatically against a clear, auditable statement.

  • Payroll-integrated deductions and loan recoveries
  • Proportionate annual profit distribution
  • One-click, fully documented final settlement
