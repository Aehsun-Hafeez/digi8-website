ECL

Expected Credit Loss

IFRS 9 impairment, modelled end to end.

Our ECL engine automates the full expected-credit-loss workflow — from staging and segmentation through to provisioning — giving you defensible numbers that stand up to audit and regulatory scrutiny.

  • Three-stage classification with configurable SICR triggers
  • PD, LGD and EAD modelling with forward-looking macro overlays
  • 12-month and lifetime ECL with scenario weighting
  • Full audit trail from raw exposure to disclosure

regs.green

The ESG operating system for audit-ready disclosures

Make sustainability goals measurable.

regs.green applies financial-close discipline to ESG data — so you can measure, report and assure your sustainability metrics with audit-ready rigour, where every number is defensible.

  • Measure — Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions via utility, fuel, fleet and procurement connectors, reconciled to your financials
  • Report — auto-map data into multiple frameworks with versioned, board-ready output and AI-assisted narrative
  • Assure — audit-complete evidence packs with full traceability, built to ISSA 5000
  • Aligned to IFRS S1/S2, CSRD/ESRS, GRI, EU Taxonomy, SFDR, CBAM
